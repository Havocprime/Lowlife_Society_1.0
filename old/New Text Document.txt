REPUTATION -
Street cred, Bussiness Acumen, 


methodology.
the first 50% of all skill levels should be relatively easily attainable.
slow but constant progression. Once 50% is complete, the grind and value session begins.
Any skill over 50% gains at significantly less rate, takes focus to develop, and has more costly
penalties for failure. 

Level 1 to Level 50 Hacking would undergo "leveling", once you hit 50, 50-100 becomes "Mastering"
Mastery is gained slower than experience, and is temporary.

Players will have an alternative stat called "Memory Retention", this can be leveled through any 
+int +wis +knowledge, action. Memory retention will determine just how long your statistics remain
unchanged before decaying.
Skill decay will be a slow but consistent curve. The longer a skill goes without use, the more it will decay.

Your highest 3 skills will have bonus defense against decay.
